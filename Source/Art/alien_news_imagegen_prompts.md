# Signals in the Noise image-generation provenance

Status: **generation complete**. The original production briefs remain below as
design history. The exact submitted prompts, selected built-in output paths, and
generation/edit mode are recorded in the executed-provenance appendix.
After reviewing each generated image, save the selected PNG at the exact
`Source/Art/Masters/<basename>.png` path shown below. The build pipeline
center-crops every master to a native `397x153` ARGB DDS news strip and also
creates a `210x176` report crop for the eight subjects marked **report + news**.
Accept only an ordinary 8-bit RGB or RGBA PNG at least `1024x512`; retain the
full-resolution built-in result rather than upscaling a smaller derivative.

The following are the original pre-generation production briefs. Exact
executed-prompt provenance follows them in the final section.
All people are fictional and anonymous. These scenes deliberately avoid
readable text, national symbols,
real political leaders, gore, logos, and watermarks so they remain reusable
across countries and alternate timelines.
