# Quiet Chorus operation art provenance

Generated with the built-in OpenAI `imagegen` tool in transparent-background image-generation/edit mode. The masters are original project assets and are transformed by `Source/Build-ArtAssets.ps1` into HOI4-native DDS sizes.

## Shared prompt contract

Use case: stylized concept. Asset type: Hearts of Iron IV alien intelligence operation UI emblem master. Existing Grey Consensus metallic-eye and cyan-signal art plus the faction's crystalline-computation art were supplied only as style and material references. Style: polished painted grand-strategy UI icon, sober science-fiction realism, crisp high-contrast silhouette, dark graphite alien metal, pale steel edges, and restrained cyan luminescence. Composition: one centered compact emblem with generous transparent padding, legible at 85x85 and 48x48. Constraints: genuine transparent alpha; no checkerboard, text, letters, numbers, flags, logos, watermark, border, box, UI frame, full spacecraft, weapons, gore, or background scene.

## Final subject prompts

- `coi_alien_operation_silent_systems_survey.png`: A sensor eye over a curved planetary horizon receiving three geometric signal echoes.
- `coi_alien_operation_seed_mimetic_access.png`: A smooth featureless human identity mask intersected by a cyan lattice key and black almond sensor eye.
- `coi_alien_operation_penetrate_research_networks.png`: A crystalline human research archive with three connected dark data prisms and an alien eye-shaped signal orbit.
- `coi_alien_operation_spoil_the_evidence.png`: An investigative document dissolving into contradictory cyan fragments beneath an alien eye.
- `coi_alien_operation_introduce_cascading_faults.png`: An infrastructure-node lattice struck by an alien pulse that causes a descending, controlled chain of fractured circuits rather than an explosion.
- `coi_alien_operation_harvest_project_archives.png`: A sealed crystalline archive core opening as cyan data streams flow into a black alien optical aperture.
- `coi_alien_operation_disrupt_research_complex.png`: A special-project laboratory tower wrapped by an interference ring with a controlled fracture through its core.
- `coi_alien_operation_extract_lead_scientist.png`: A small human scientist in a lab coat lifted intact through three cyan rings toward an alien eye; clinical and nonviolent.

For Silent Systems Survey, Spoil the Evidence, and Extract Lead Scientist, a second imagegen background-extraction edit was used to replace unwanted generated checkerboard pixels with genuine alpha while preserving the emblem itself.
